How This App Works

Classes

Create one entry per Core with + New Class. Each class has its own roster, tables, seating, locks, rules, and saved charts — switching the Class dropdown swaps everything at once. Rename Class changes the name without touching anything else. Set your Teacher Name once at the top of the toolbar; it's saved automatically and appears on every PDF. Delete Class removes the class and everything in it, and can't be undone.

Three views

  • Clean View (the default, and what ✅ Done returns you to) — a tidy, read-only look at the chart.
  • 📐 Edit Layout — the table tools: Saved Layout, Quick Grid, Select Tables / Duplicate. Every table can be dragged, and each one shows a toolbar strip along its bottom.
  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Edit Students — the student tools: Shuffle, Seating Rules, Kagan Group Import, and Roster. Tables stay put, each seat shows its own toolbar strip, and you can drag students from Unassigned onto open seats.

Saved Seating Charts, Seat Numbers, Export/Print PDF, Seat Roster Report, and this Cheat Sheet are available in every view.

Saved Seating Charts

A saved chart is a snapshot of the whole room: the tables, who sits where, which seats are locked, and where the Teacher Desk is. Use it when you switch between arrangements — say, lab groups normally and rows for testing.

  • 💾 Save Current Chart... asks for a name (like "Lab groups") and saves the room exactly as it is.
  • Restore puts the chosen chart back. Your current room is auto-saved first, so restoring never loses anything. Students added since the chart was saved land in Unassigned; students removed from the roster are skipped.
  • Automatic saves (⏱): before Quick Grid, applying a Saved Layout, Clear Layout, or a Restore replaces your tables, the app saves the current chart for you. The 10 most recent automatic saves per class are kept.
  • Delete removes a saved chart. It doesn't change the room you're looking at.
Switching to rows for a test: 💾 save your groups chart (or just let the ⏱ auto-save catch it), build or apply your rows, and when testing is over, pick the groups chart and click Restore.

Saved Layout (in Edit Layout mode) is different: it saves only the tables, so you can reuse the same arrangement in any class. Applying one clears that class's seating.

Adding students

  • Type a name, pick M/F/- (or leave it blank), then click + Add Student or press Enter.
  • Upload CSV (Roster section, Edit Students mode): Last, First, Gender(M/F), one student per line. A header row is detected and skipped. Uploading adds students — click Clear Roster first to replace the list.
  • Click the (M)/(F)/(-) badge on an unassigned student to change their gender.
  • Click a student's name (in Unassigned, or on a seat in Edit Students mode) to rename them. Renaming doesn't move them.
  • Kagan Group Import opens a companion tool in a new tab with the same sign-in. Give it names and test scores, and it builds balanced 4-person Kagan teams and seats them into this class's existing pods.

Seating rules

  • Keep-Apart Groups — check 2 or more students who should never share a table or sit at touching tables. Make as many groups as you need.
  • 🎯 Front Row & Near Teacher — check off students who need the front row, need to be near your desk, or both. Front row means the row of seats closest to the bottom of the chart (the front of the room). Near teacher means within about two seats of the Teacher Desk box. Drag that box in Edit Layout mode to where your desk really is.
  • Students with front-row or near-teacher needs show a small dark label (FRONT / NEAR T) on their seat in Edit Students mode only. It never prints, so IEP/504 needs stay private.
  • When a rule is broken — a keep-apart pair sitting together, or a student away from the front or the teacher — that seat gets a red outline and a ⚠️ in the corner. Hover the ⚠️ to see which rule.

Shuffling and fixing (Edit Students mode)

  • 📌 Place Students fills every unlocked seat with the unlocked students. Front-row and near-teacher students are seated first, then everyone else fills in from the front of the room to the back, steering around keep-apart groups. If the room is too tight to follow every rule, it tells you how many seats were affected.
  • Order: Random (default), or Alphabetical by first or last name.
  • Alternate gender interleaves boys and girls. Random order only.
  • Avoid recent neighbors remembers who sat next to whom in your last 3 charts and tries not to seat them together again. Random order only. Each time you click Place Students, the chart you're replacing — including any changes you dragged in by hand — is added to that memory.
  • 🔧 Fix Rule Breaks leaves your chart alone except for the students breaking a rule. It makes the smallest swaps it can to fix them and tells you how many students moved. Locked students are never moved. If something can't be fixed (say, more front-row students than front seats), it tells you what's left.
  • Clear Seats sends everyone in an unlocked seat back to Unassigned. Locked students stay put.

Seat toolbar (Edit Students mode)

Each seat is a square: the top three quarters show the name, and the bottom quarter is a toolbar.

  • 🔒 / 🔓 (left) locks or unlocks the seat, filled or empty. Locked seats turn gold.
  • Undo arrow (right, filled and unlocked seats only) sends that student back to Unassigned — handy when drag-and-drop is fussy on a Chromebook touchscreen.
  • 🔓 Clear All Locks unlocks every seat in the class without moving anyone.

Locked means locked. A locked student never moves until you unlock them. Place Students, Fix Rule Breaks, and Clear Seats all skip them, and any layout change that would remove their seat (Quick Grid, applying a Saved Layout, Clear Layout, removing their table, or shrinking its seat count) is stopped with a message listing who's in the way. Restoring a saved chart asks you first, then unlocks them and uses that chart's seats and locks instead. Moving or rotating a table is fine — the student rides along with it.

IEP / 504 seating: check the student's need in 🎯 Front Row & Near Teacher and let Place Students handle it, or put them in the exact seat they need and click 🔒. Everyone else fills in around them.

Building tables (Edit Layout mode)

  • Quick Grid: choose rows × columns and seats per desk (1 = single desks, 2 = lab tables), then Generate. This replaces the tables and clears seating, but never touches the roster. The old chart is auto-saved first.
  • + Add Desk adds one single desk. Clear Layout removes every table (students return to Unassigned).
  • Table toolbar (bottom of each table), left to right: rotate 90°, seats (chair icon with the seat count, 1–6), and × to remove the table. Rotating turns the table around its center, so you may need to nudge it back into place.
  • Select Tables: On lets you click tables to select them (blue outline). Drag one and the whole selection moves together. Duplicate Selected copies the selection just to the right and selects the copies, so you can keep duplicating. Clear Selection deselects.
  • While dragging, blue guide lines appear when edges or centers line up with another table, and the table snaps into place.
  • Teacher Desk box: drag it to where your desk really is. The Near teacher rule measures from it.

Exporting

Export/Print PDF lets you choose Landscape, Portrait, or Auto (whichever prints your tables biggest). The PDF is cropped to your tables and scaled to fill the page, with the class name, your teacher name, and the date and time at the top. Seats are shaded by gender (blue for boys, green for girls). Locks, ⚠️ warnings, Front/Near-teacher labels, toolbars, and the Teacher Desk box don't print. If 🔢 Seat Numbers is on, the seat numbers print too.

🔢 Seat Numbers numbers every seat top-to-bottom, then left-to-right. The 🖨️ Seat Roster Report uses the same numbers: pick the classes that share a layout (e.g. Core 1–4) for a table with one row per seat and a column per class, or print 🏷️ Desk Labels on Avery 58163 sheets (2" × 4", 10 per sheet) showing each seat number and who sits there in each class.

Seat Roster Report

Pick the classes that share this same desk layout (e.g. Core 1–4). Each physical seat becomes one row, with a column per class showing who sits there.

Keep-Apart Groups

Anyone checked together below will never be placed at the same table or in a touching seat by 📌 Place Students. You can still drag them together yourself — that seat will just show a ⚠️ warning as a reminder.

Front Row & Near Teacher

Check what each student needs. 📌 Place Students seats these students first, and 🔧 Fix Rule Breaks moves them back if they end up in the wrong spot. Front row is the row of seats closest to the bottom of the chart. Near teacher is within about two seats of the Teacher Desk box, which you can drag in Edit Layout mode. These marks show only in Edit Students mode and never print.

Migrate Old Data

1. Open Firebase Console → Realtime Database in a new tab (you're already signed into that as yourself — no need to sign in again here).
2. Under seatingCharts, click to expand and open one specific ID — the one holding your old classes (it'll show classes, rosters, etc. directly inside it once you're on that ID's row). Don't export the seatingCharts node itself — go one level deeper, into that specific ID.
3. With that ID's row selected/highlighted, click the ⋮ menu → Export JSON — this downloads a file for just that one ID.
4. Open that file, copy everything, and paste it below.
